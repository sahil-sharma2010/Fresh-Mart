Order Cancellation Information

Important things to know before requesting a cancellation.

Request Early

If you need to cancel, submit your request as soon as possible. An order may become harder to cancel once processing has started.

Order Status Matters

Cancellation availability may depend on whether the order is pending, being prepared, dispatched or already delivered.

Contact Support

If a cancellation option is not available, contact FreshMart support with your order number and cancellation request.

Refund Information

If payment has already been collected, any eligible refund is handled according to the applicable refund policy and payment method.

Confirmation

Do not assume an order is cancelled until you receive confirmation or the order status clearly reflects the cancellation.

Keep Order Details

Keep your order number and relevant confirmation messages available so the support team can review your request.

Important information

Cancellation eligibility, any applicable charges and refund handling depend on the actual order status and FreshMart's applicable policies. Check the details shown on the website and contact support if you are unsure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common cancellation questions.

How can I cancel my FreshMart order?

Open your order details and use the cancellation option if it is available. If you cannot find the option, contact support with your order number and request cancellation.

Can I cancel an order after it has been placed?

Cancellation may be possible depending on the current order status. Request it as early as possible. Once an order is being prepared or dispatched, cancellation may no longer be available.

Can I cancel an order that has already been dispatched?

Cancellation after dispatch may be restricted. Contact support to check the available options. Do not assume the order is cancelled until you receive confirmation.

Will I receive a refund after cancellation?

If payment has been collected and your cancellation is eligible for a refund, it will be handled according to the applicable refund policy and payment method. Review the Refund Policy for details.

How long will a refund take?

Refund processing time can depend on the payment method and the payment provider. Check the refund information shared for your order or contact support for an update. Do not rely on an unconfirmed processing deadline.

What if I cannot find the cancellation button?

The option may depend on your order's current status. Contact FreshMart support using the contact details below and provide your order number.

Can I cancel only one item from an order?

Partial cancellation depends on the options supported for your order. Contact support to ask whether an individual item can be cancelled before the order is processed further.

What if I receive a cancellation confirmation by mistake?

Check your order status and contact support promptly if the confirmation does not match your request. Share the order number and confirmation details so the issue can be reviewed.

Can I place a new order after cancellation?

You can place a new order if the products are available and delivery is supported for your address. Review your cart and order details before confirming the new purchase.

Need Help Cancelling an Order?

Contact FreshMart support with your order number and cancellation request. Never share your password, OTP or payment PIN.
