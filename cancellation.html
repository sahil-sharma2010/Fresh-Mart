How to Cancel Order Status Refunds Unavailable Items FAQs

1. Introduction

This Cancellation Policy explains the general process for requesting cancellation of a FreshMart order. Whether an order can be cancelled depends on its status, the services actually available and applicable law.

2. How to Request Cancellation

  1. Keep your order number or order details ready, if available.
  2. Contact FreshMart through phone, email or WhatsApp.
  3. Clearly state that you want to request cancellation.
  4. Provide any additional information reasonably needed to identify the order.
  5. Wait for confirmation of the actual order status and next steps.

Sending a cancellation request does not by itself mean the order has been cancelled. This page provides instructions and does not submit a cancellation request automatically.

3. Cancellation Based on Order Status

Before processing: A request may be easier to accommodate when order preparation has not started, but approval depends on the actual order system and circumstances.

During preparation or packing: The order may already involve picking and packing products. Contact support as soon as possible to ask whether cancellation is possible.

After dispatch: Cancellation may be more limited once an order has been dispatched for delivery. Contact FreshMart to understand the available options. Any applicable legal rights remain unaffected.

4. Cancellation Confirmation

Customers should rely on a genuine confirmation from the actual order system or FreshMart support rather than assuming that a message has completed the cancellation. If an order-status feature is not available, contact support for clarification.

5. Prepaid Orders and Refunds

If a prepaid order is cancelled and a refund is due, the refund will be handled through the payment process actually supported by FreshMart. Eligibility depends on the circumstances and applicable law.

Processing time may depend on the payment provider. This policy does not promise a fixed refund timeline. See the Return & Refund Policy for additional information.

6. Cash on Delivery Orders

For Cash on Delivery orders, contact support to ask about cancellation based on the current order status. If no payment was collected, a payment refund may not apply; any other issue will be reviewed according to the actual order circumstances.

7. Out-of-Stock or Unavailable Products

If an item becomes unavailable, FreshMart should communicate the available options where reasonably possible. Depending on the order system, these may include an order update, cancellation or an eligible refund.

A substitute product should not be assumed unless it is accepted by the customer where required.

8. Cancellation by FreshMart

In some circumstances, an order may not be fulfillable because of product availability, service limitations, an unresolved payment issue or another legitimate operational reason. FreshMart should communicate relevant information and handle any eligible refund according to applicable policy and law.

9. Partial Cancellation

Cancelling only one item from an order depends on whether the ordering system supports partial cancellation. If this feature is unavailable, contact FreshMart to ask about the options for your particular order.

10. Cancellation Charges

This page does not establish a cancellation fee or penalty. Any charge, if applicable, must be part of the actual disclosed order terms and comply with applicable law.

11. Customer Rights

This policy must be read consistently with applicable Indian laws and consumer-protection requirements. Nothing in it is intended to remove a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted.

12. Policy Updates

FreshMart may update this policy when its services or operational processes change. The current version should be published on this page.

13. Need Help Cancelling an Order?

Contact FreshMart with your order details to ask about cancellation eligibility and the current order status.
