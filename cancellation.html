Cancellation Policy

FreshMart understands that plans can change. This Cancellation Policy explains how customers can request order cancellations, how order processing affects cancellation requests, and how eligible refunds may be handled.

Effective date: 29 October 2026

01

Policy Overview

This Cancellation Policy describes how cancellation requests for orders placed through the FreshMart website may be handled. It should be read together with our Terms & Conditions, Delivery Policy and Return & Refund Policy.

Whether an order can be cancelled may depend on its current status, whether products have been packed or dispatched, payment status and the circumstances of the request.

Important: Submit your cancellation request as soon as possible. A request is not confirmed until FreshMart communicates that the cancellation has been accepted.

This policy does not remove any mandatory rights available to customers under applicable consumer protection laws.

02

Customer-Initiated Cancellation

Customers who no longer need an order may contact FreshMart to request cancellation. We recommend making the request before the order enters packing or dispatch.

A cancellation request should include:

  • Your order number or order reference.
  • The name or contact details used when placing the order.
  • The products or order you want to cancel.
  • A brief reason for cancellation, if you wish to provide one.

FreshMart will review the request against the current order status and inform you of the available next steps.

Do not assume that an order has been cancelled merely because you submitted a message or stopped using the website.

03

Order Processing Stages

Cancellation options can differ depending on how far the order has progressed through the fulfilment process.

  • Order placed: Contact FreshMart immediately to request cancellation.
  • Order processing: Cancellation may be possible if preparation has not progressed too far.
  • Order packed: The order may already be prepared for dispatch, so cancellation requires confirmation.
  • Order dispatched: A standard pre-dispatch cancellation may no longer be possible. Contact support for guidance on available options.
  • Order delivered: Refer to the Return & Refund Policy for eligible complaints or remedies.

These stages describe general order handling. The exact statuses available depend on the features implemented on the FreshMart website.

04

How to Request a Cancellation

To request cancellation, follow these steps:

  1. Locate your order confirmation or order number.
  2. Open the Help & Support page or use the published store contact details.
  3. Provide your order number and explain that you want to cancel.
  4. Wait for FreshMart to review the current order status.
  5. Keep the cancellation confirmation for your records if the request is accepted.

Visit Help & Support to find the contact options published by FreshMart.

Please note: The existence of this policy does not mean an automatic cancellation feature is available. Use only the cancellation or support options actually provided by the website.

05

Cancellation Confirmation

A cancellation request must be reviewed before it can be treated as completed. FreshMart should communicate the outcome through an available contact or order-status channel.

If the request is accepted, retain any confirmation message, reference number or updated order status provided to you.

If the request cannot be completed because the order has already been packed or dispatched, contact support to discuss the available options.

Customers should not rely on an unconfirmed cancellation when making decisions about an expected delivery.

06

Cancellation of Prepaid Orders

Prepaid orders are orders for which payment has been made online or through another supported payment method before delivery.

If a prepaid order is successfully cancelled and a refund is due, the refund will generally be initiated through the original payment method where supported and appropriate.

  • Keep your order number and payment reference available.
  • Allow time for payment verification where required.
  • Check the relevant bank, card, UPI or payment-provider account for updates.
  • Contact support if a confirmed refund appears delayed.

The time needed for a refund to appear can depend on the payment provider and banking process. No fixed processing time is guaranteed unless FreshMart has explicitly confirmed one.

07

Cancellation of Cash on Delivery Orders

For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, payment is generally collected when the order is delivered, subject to the options offered at checkout.

If a COD order has not been dispatched, contact FreshMart promptly to request cancellation.

If the order has already been dispatched, contact support to understand whether cancellation or another arrangement is available.

If you have not paid for the order, there is generally no product payment to refund. However, any amount already collected or separately paid must be reviewed fairly where a refund is due.

Any restrictions or additional charges must be disclosed clearly and applied consistently with applicable law.

08

Cancellations Initiated by FreshMart

In some circumstances, FreshMart may be unable to fulfil an order and may need to contact the customer about cancellation.

Possible reasons may include:

  • A product becoming unavailable after the order was placed.
  • An address outside the supported delivery area.
  • An unresolved payment or order-verification issue.
  • A material product listing or pricing error requiring review.
  • Operational disruption that prevents fulfilment.
  • A safety or legal restriction affecting the order.

Where an accepted, paid order is cancelled by FreshMart and a refund is due, the applicable refund should be arranged according to the circumstances and applicable law.

FreshMart should explain the reason for cancellation and the available next steps where reasonably possible.

09

Out-of-Stock or Unavailable Products

Product availability can change between browsing, checkout and order preparation. A product displayed on the website may occasionally become unavailable before fulfilment.

If an item cannot be supplied, FreshMart may contact the customer to discuss an appropriate resolution, depending on the order and available options.

Possible resolutions may include:

  • Removing or cancelling the unavailable item, where appropriate.
  • Discussing an alternative product if the customer agrees.
  • Adjusting the order total where an item is not supplied.
  • Arranging a refund for an eligible amount already paid.
  • Cancelling the full order if it cannot reasonably be fulfilled.

FreshMart should not substitute a product without appropriate customer agreement where such agreement is required.

10

Incorrect, Duplicate or Accidental Orders

If you accidentally place an order, select the wrong quantity or notice a duplicate order, contact FreshMart as soon as possible.

Include the order number or numbers and explain what needs to be corrected. Each order may need to be reviewed separately.

If an order has not yet been prepared or dispatched, it may be possible to cancel it or correct the details.

If the order has already been dispatched or delivered, the available options may be different. Refer to the Return & Refund Policy where relevant.

If a duplicate payment appears on your bank or payment account, keep the payment references and contact support for verification.

11

Orders Already Packed or Dispatched

Once an order has been packed or handed to a delivery service, stopping the delivery may not always be possible.

If you want to cancel an order at this stage, contact FreshMart and provide the order number. Support can explain the options available for that order.

Do not assume that a dispatched order has been cancelled until you receive confirmation.

If the order has already been delivered, a cancellation request may instead need to be assessed under the return, refund, damaged-product or incorrect-delivery process.

Any handling fees or deductions must have a lawful basis, be properly disclosed and comply with applicable consumer rights.

12

Refund Processing After Cancellation

If a cancellation is accepted and a refund is due, FreshMart will review the payment status and arrange the applicable refund.

Refund handling may depend on the original payment method, payment-provider procedures, bank processing and whether the transaction has been successfully settled.

  • Keep the order confirmation and payment reference.
  • Check your original payment account for refund updates.
  • Allow the relevant payment provider time to process the transaction.
  • Contact support if you need help tracing a confirmed refund.

A payment marked pending or failed may need verification before FreshMart or the payment provider can confirm whether a refund or reversal is required.

Refund timing: Any timeline communicated by FreshMart should be treated according to its stated conditions. Bank and payment-provider processing may take additional time.

For more details, read the Return & Refund Policy.

13

Discounts, Coupons and Promotional Offers

If an order uses a discount code, coupon, promotional price or free-delivery offer, cancellation may affect the promotion according to its published terms.

  • A coupon may have eligibility conditions or an expiry date.
  • A promotion may require a minimum eligible order value.
  • Cancellation of part of an order may change the remaining order total.
  • Refund calculations should reflect the amount actually paid and the applicable offer terms.

FreshMart should explain any relevant adjustment where reasonably possible. Promotional terms must not be used to remove mandatory customer rights under applicable law.

Please review the offer conditions shown when the promotion is applied to your order.

14

Exceptions and Customer Rights

Some products or order circumstances may require special handling because of hygiene, safety, perishability, customisation or applicable legal requirements.

Any restriction on cancellation, return or refund should be communicated clearly where required. A restriction does not automatically apply to every product or situation.

This policy should be interpreted consistently with applicable Indian laws, including consumer protection requirements where relevant.

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude a remedy or right that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.

FreshMart may update this policy when its services or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page, with any required notice handled appropriately.

15

Cancellation Help and Customer Support

If you need to cancel an order, have a question about a cancellation request or need help with a refund, use the contact details or support options published on FreshMart.

To help with your request, provide your order number, the registered contact details and a short explanation of the issue.

Visit Help & Support

FreshMart aims to review cancellation concerns fairly and explain the next steps. If a complaint remains unresolved, customers may use the applicable consumer grievance or dispute-resolution mechanisms available under law.

For information about delivery arrangements, read our Delivery Policy.

For general website rules, read our Terms & Conditions.
