Account & Website Protection

Security Policy

FreshMart aims to protect customer accounts and information through appropriate website security practices. This policy explains account passwords, forgotten password recovery, registered email and mobile number changes, verification, suspicious activity and safe account use.

01

Security Policy Overview

This Security Policy describes the account protection and website-security practices relevant to FreshMart customers. It covers passwords, account recovery, verification and the responsible handling of security-related reports.

The actual security features available to customers depend on the functionality implemented on the FreshMart website and its connected services.

Security reminder: Never share your password, login verification code or password-reset link with another person. FreshMart customers should use only the official website and verified support channels.

For information about how customer information is collected and handled, read our Privacy Policy.

02

Password Requirements and Safety

A strong password helps protect a FreshMart account against unauthorized access. Customers should create a password that is difficult for another person to guess.

Recommended password practices include:

  • Use a long password, preferably at least 12 characters.
  • Combine letters, numbers and symbols where supported.
  • Avoid common words, predictable patterns and easily guessed information.
  • Do not reuse your FreshMart password on other websites.
  • Do not share your password with friends, strangers or unofficial support accounts.
  • Change your password if you believe someone else may know it.

If a password manager is used, keep it protected with a strong master password and any available additional authentication.

FreshMart should never ask customers to send their current password through email, chat or a support message.

03

How to Change Your Password

Customers should use the account settings or password-change option provided on the official FreshMart website, where available.

General password-change steps:

  1. Open the official FreshMart website.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open Account Settings, Profile or Security Settings, if available.
  4. Select the Change Password option.
  5. Complete any current-password or verification step requested by the system.
  6. Enter and confirm your new password.
  7. Save the change and follow any confirmation instructions displayed.

After changing your password: If you suspect someone else had access to your account, review your account activity and sign out of other sessions if that option exists.

These instructions describe a typical process. The exact menu names and available options depend on the features implemented on FreshMart.

04

Forgot Password and Email Recovery

If you forget your password, use the Forgot Password or Reset Password option on the official FreshMart login page, if available.

General password-recovery steps:

  1. Open the FreshMart login page.
  2. Select Forgot Password or the equivalent recovery option.
  3. Enter the registered email address or other identifier requested.
  4. Check the registered email inbox for recovery instructions, if email recovery is enabled.
  5. Check the spam or junk folder if the message does not appear.
  6. Open the genuine reset link and follow the instructions to create a new password.
  7. Return to the login page and sign in with the new password.

Stay safe: Do not forward password-reset links or share verification codes. If you did not request a reset, do not use an unexpected link; review your account security and contact official support if necessary.

Password-reset links should be handled securely and may expire. If a link has expired, request a new one through the official recovery process.

Email-based recovery will work only if the website has a properly configured password-reset system and email delivery.

05

Changing Your Registered Email Address

A registered email address may be used for account communication, password recovery, order updates or verification, depending on the services enabled on FreshMart.

If an email-change option is available, customers should use the account settings provided on the official website.

  1. Sign in to your FreshMart account.
  2. Open Profile or Account Settings, if available.
  3. Select the email address change option.
  4. Enter the new email address carefully.
  5. Complete any verification requested by the website.
  6. Check the new inbox for a confirmation message, if email verification is enabled.
  7. Confirm the change using the genuine instructions provided.

Customers should use an email account they control and can access. A change may require additional verification to reduce the risk of unauthorized account takeover.

If you no longer have access to your registered email address, use the official support or account-recovery process. FreshMart may need to verify the request before updating account details.

Do not send email passwords or email-account recovery codes to FreshMart support.

06

Changing Your Registered Mobile Number

If FreshMart supports mobile-number updates, customers should use the account settings or official account-recovery process.

General mobile-number change steps:

  1. Sign in to the official FreshMart website.
  2. Open Profile or Account Settings, if available.
  3. Choose the mobile-number update option.
  4. Enter the new number in the format requested.
  5. Complete the verification step offered by the website.
  6. Confirm that the updated number is displayed correctly, if the site provides this information.

Lost or changed number: If you cannot receive a verification code on your old number, use the official recovery process. Do not attempt to access another person's number or account.

FreshMart may need to verify ownership of the account before changing a registered number. Verification requirements should be reasonable and handled securely.

A mobile-number change feature is not automatically available just because it is described in this policy. It must be implemented in the account system.

07

OTP and Verification Code Security

A one-time password (OTP) or verification code may be used to confirm an action or verify access to an account, email address or mobile number where supported.

  • Enter a verification code only on the genuine website or app that requested it.
  • Never share an OTP with another person.
  • Do not share codes with callers claiming to be delivery agents, support staff or administrators.
  • Request a new code only through the official verification process.
  • If a code expires, follow the website's instructions to request another one.
  • Report repeated unexpected verification messages if you suspect misuse.

Customers should not assume that every FreshMart account action uses OTP verification. Available verification methods depend on the website's actual implementation.

FreshMart should never ask a customer to reveal an OTP so that staff can sign in to the customer's account on their behalf.

08

Login and Account Access Security

Customers should access their accounts through the official FreshMart website and keep their login information confidential.

  • Check that you are using the correct website address before signing in.
  • Avoid entering passwords on suspicious links received through messages or emails.
  • Use a trusted device and keep its browser and operating system updated.
  • Do not save passwords on public or shared computers.
  • Sign out after using a shared device.
  • Do not allow unknown people to use your signed-in account.

If you believe someone has accessed your account without permission, change your password using the official process and contact support if further assistance is needed.

Account protections such as multi-factor authentication, login alerts or automatic session expiry should be described as available only when they are actually implemented.

09

Suspicious Activity and Unauthorized Access

Customers should report unusual account activity as soon as reasonably possible. Examples may include:

  • Unexpected password-reset messages.
  • Changes to account information that you did not make.
  • Orders or transactions you do not recognize.
  • Unexpected verification codes or repeated login attempts.
  • Messages requesting passwords, OTPs or sensitive payment details.
  • Suspicious links or people impersonating FreshMart support.

If you suspect unauthorized account access:

  1. Do not click suspicious links or share verification codes.
  2. Change your password through the official website if you can still access your account.
  3. Review recent orders and account information where those features are available.
  4. Contact FreshMart through its published support channel.
  5. If a payment account may also be affected, contact the relevant bank or payment provider directly.

FreshMart should assess reported incidents and take reasonable steps appropriate to the nature of the issue and the capabilities of its systems.

10

Logout, Devices and Active Sessions

Customers should sign out after using a shared computer or a device that other people can access.

If FreshMart provides a list of active sessions or signed-in devices, customers should review it when they suspect unusual activity and end sessions they do not recognize.

  • Use the website's Logout option when finishing a shared session.
  • Close shared browser windows after signing out.
  • Avoid saving passwords on public computers.
  • Protect personal devices with a screen lock where available.
  • Keep your browser and device security updates current.

Clearing browser data or closing a tab does not necessarily guarantee that every account session has ended.

Remote logout, session management and device lists are available only if supported by the FreshMart account system.

11

Account Recovery and Verification

If you lose access to your account, use the official password recovery or support process available on FreshMart.

Depending on the situation and available functionality, recovery may involve confirming access to a registered email address, verifying a mobile number or completing another reasonable account-ownership check.

If you no longer control your registered email or phone number, explain this through the official support channel. Do not submit passwords or OTPs as proof of identity.

FreshMart may be unable to complete a change immediately if additional verification is needed to protect the account.

Recovery instructions should be shared through appropriate channels and should not expose an existing password or unnecessary customer information.

12

Customer Data and Payment Security

FreshMart aims to handle account information and order-related data responsibly. Access to such information should be limited according to the website's operational needs and applicable law.

Customers should use the payment methods made available through the website and review payment details before confirming a transaction.

  • Never send a card PIN, banking password or OTP through support messages.
  • Do not make payments to an unfamiliar account based on an unsolicited message.
  • Check order totals and payment details before confirming a purchase.
  • Contact your bank or payment provider directly if you notice an unauthorized transaction.
  • Use official support options to report suspicious FreshMart payment requests.

Payment security depends in part on the payment provider and the payment methods integrated with FreshMart. No payment security certification or encryption standard is claimed here unless it has been independently verified.

Read our Privacy Policy for more information about customer information handling.

13

Website Security and Safe Usage

Customers should use the FreshMart website lawfully and should not attempt to interfere with its operation or access information belonging to other users.

Prohibited activities include:

  • Attempting to access another person's account without permission.
  • Trying to bypass authentication or verification controls.
  • Submitting malicious code or attempting to disrupt the website.
  • Misusing account recovery or verification features.
  • Using another person's account or payment details without authorization.
  • Attempting to access restricted administrative functions without permission.

Suspected website vulnerabilities or security problems should be reported through the official support channel. Do not exploit a weakness or access another customer's information to prove it.

FreshMart may review reported issues and take reasonable steps to investigate and address them.

14

Security Limitations and Policy Updates

FreshMart aims to use appropriate safeguards, but no website, device, network or online service can be guaranteed to be completely free from security risks.

Security features vary according to the actual website configuration, third-party services and technical functionality available at a given time.

This policy does not guarantee that every account has password reset by email, OTP verification, mobile-number updates, login alerts, active-session management or multi-factor authentication. Those features must be implemented and configured before customers can use them.

FreshMart may revise this policy as its services, security practices or applicable requirements change. Updated terms will be published on this page.

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights or responsibilities that apply under law.

15

Security Questions and Customer Support

If you suspect unauthorized account access, cannot recover your account or notice a suspicious FreshMart message, use the contact details or support options published on the website.

Include a short description of the issue and your order reference, if relevant. Never include your password, OTP, banking PIN or payment-account password.
