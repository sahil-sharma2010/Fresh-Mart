Policy Overview

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used on the FreshMart website to support website operation, remember preferences and understand website usage.

The actual cookies used depend on the website features, hosting configuration and third-party services that are enabled. This policy does not mean that every type of cookie described here is currently active.

Your choice matters: Where required, non-essential cookies should be used only after the appropriate consent has been obtained. You may manage available options through the website's cookie controls, if implemented.

For information about personal information, read our Privacy Policy.

02

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store in a browser or device. They can help a website remember information between visits or maintain certain website functions.

Similar technologies may include browser storage, pixels or scripts that perform related functions. Their use depends on the technology and purpose involved.

  • Some cookies help a website function correctly.
  • Some remember preferences such as language or display choices.
  • Some help measure website performance, where enabled.
  • Some third-party services may use technologies for their own stated purposes.
03

How Cookies May Be Used

Depending on the features enabled on FreshMart, cookies may be used to support:

  • Basic website navigation and technical functions.
  • Remembering customer preferences.
  • Maintaining certain shopping or session functions, if supported.
  • Understanding website performance and errors, where analytics are enabled.
  • Security, fraud prevention or service reliability where appropriate.

FreshMart should not use cookies for purposes that are inconsistent with applicable law or the consent choices required for those purposes.

04

Types of Cookies

Cookies can be grouped according to their purpose and duration.

  • Session cookies: Usually removed when the browser session ends.
  • Persistent cookies: Remain for a defined period or until removed.
  • First-party cookies: Set by the website being visited.
  • Third-party cookies: Set by another service integrated into a website.

These categories describe common cookie types; they do not confirm that every category is used by FreshMart.

05

Essential and Functional Cookies

Essential cookies may be needed for requested website functions, security or session management. Without them, certain features may not work as intended.

Depending on the implementation, functional technologies may support selected preferences or remember settings chosen by the customer.

Where applicable law permits strictly necessary cookies without consent, they should still be used only for the relevant necessary purpose. Other cookies should be handled according to the applicable consent requirements.

06

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies may remember choices such as display preferences or other settings, if those features exist on the website.

They can help reduce the need to re-enter the same preferences on later visits. If preference cookies are not implemented, the website may not remember those choices automatically.

07

Analytics and Performance Cookies

Analytics technologies may help website operators understand general patterns, such as pages visited, loading performance or technical errors.

FreshMart should describe any analytics provider actually used and obtain consent where required before activating non-essential analytics technologies.

This policy does not claim that a particular analytics service is installed or active.

08

Advertising and Marketing Cookies

Some websites use advertising technologies to measure campaigns or personalize advertisements. These technologies should be used on FreshMart only if they are actually integrated and permitted by applicable law.

Where consent is required, marketing cookies should remain disabled until the required consent is given. Customers should be able to change their choices through the available controls where required.

09

Third-Party Services

FreshMart may rely on external providers for services such as payment processing, hosting, embedded content or analytics. Some providers may use cookies or similar technologies as part of their service.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy and cookie policies. FreshMart should identify relevant providers when appropriate and review their use of technologies before integration.

External websites are governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

10

How Long Cookies Remain

Cookie duration varies by purpose and configuration. Session cookies typically last for a browser session, while persistent cookies may remain until their expiry date or until deleted.

The exact duration of any cookie should be based on the actual implementation and should not be guessed. Customers can use browser controls to inspect or remove cookies stored on their devices.

11

Managing Cookie Preferences

If FreshMart provides a cookie preference panel, customers may use it to review and update available categories of non-essential cookies.

  • Accept or reject optional categories where those choices are offered.
  • Change previously selected preferences through the available controls.
  • Review the purpose of each category before making a choice.

Use Cookie Settings if that page and its preference controls are implemented. A settings page alone does not create a working consent manager.

12

Browser Cookie Controls

Most modern browsers provide settings for viewing, blocking or deleting cookies. The exact instructions depend on the browser and device.

  • Open your browser's Settings or Privacy section.
  • Find the Cookies or Site Data controls.
  • Review or remove stored website data as desired.
  • Use the browser's help documentation for detailed instructions.

Blocking all cookies may affect some website functions, including features that rely on sessions or saved preferences.

13

Consent and Your Choices

Where consent is legally required, optional cookies should not be activated before valid consent is provided. Customers should be able to withdraw or change consent through an appropriate mechanism.

Necessary website functions may be treated differently where applicable law allows. Rejecting optional cookies should not be used to remove rights that customers have under law.

The available controls depend on the consent-management functionality implemented on FreshMart.

14

Changes to This Cookie Policy

FreshMart may update this policy if website features, providers, technologies or legal requirements change.

Revised information will be published on this page. Where required, FreshMart will request consent again or provide an appropriate notice before applying material changes to optional cookie use.

Customers should review this page periodically to understand current practices.

15

Cookie Questions and Support

If you have questions about cookies or cannot access the available preference controls, use the contact details or support options published by FreshMart.
