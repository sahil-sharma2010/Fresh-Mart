Return & Refund Policy

At FreshMart, we want you to receive the products you ordered in the expected condition. This policy explains how to report damaged, defective, expired, incorrect or missing items, request eligible returns and understand refund processing.

Effective date: 29 October 2026

01

Policy Overview

This Return & Refund Policy applies to purchases made through the FreshMart website, subject to the terms shown for the relevant product and order.

Return eligibility depends on the product, its condition, the reason for the complaint, the available evidence and applicable consumer-protection law.

Our commitment: If you receive an incorrect, damaged, defective, expired or incomplete order, report the issue so FreshMart can review it and determine an appropriate resolution under this policy and applicable law.

Depending on the situation, the resolution may include a replacement, delivery of a missing item, a refund for an eligible item, a partial refund or another mutually agreed solution where permitted.

This policy does not guarantee that every product can be returned or that every complaint will qualify for a refund. However, restrictions in this policy do not remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

02

Products Eligible for a Complaint

You may contact FreshMart if you experience one or more of the following problems with an order:

  • Damaged product: The product or packaging arrives damaged, leaking, broken or unusable.
  • Wrong product: You receive a different product, brand, size, variant or item from the one ordered.
  • Missing product: An item listed in the confirmed order is not included in the delivered package.
  • Expired product: A product is delivered past its printed expiry date or use-by date.
  • Quality issue: A product has a credible quality or safety concern, subject to verification and applicable law.
  • Incorrect quantity: The delivered quantity is less than the quantity confirmed in the order.
  • Order discrepancy: The item delivered materially differs from the confirmed order details.

Please keep the product, packaging, label and order details available until the complaint has been reviewed, unless the product is unsafe to keep.

03

When Should You Report a Problem?

For faster investigation, FreshMart recommends reporting visible damage, wrong items, missing items or other delivery discrepancies as soon as possible after delivery.

Suggested reporting window: Aim to report delivery-related problems within 24 hours of receiving your order, where reasonably possible. This is a proposed operational target and must be confirmed before publication.

If you discover an expiry, safety or other issue later, contact support promptly after discovering it. Explain when and how the issue became apparent.

A reporting window helps us investigate, but it is not intended to remove any mandatory legal rights or remedies. A complaint will be assessed according to the circumstances and applicable law.

04

Mandatory Evidence & Information Required (4 Photos & 1 Video)

To ensure fast, fair and transparent verification of every return or complaint request on FreshMart, customers must provide complete order details along with clear visual proof through our Return Order Portal:

  • Customer & Order Details: Customer Name (as per order), valid FreshMart Order Number (e.g. FM-9901), 10-digit Mobile Number, Email Address and complete Order Delivery Address.
  • Complaint Description: A clear description of the issue (up to 500 characters) selecting the relevant category (Damaged Product Received, Wrong Product Delivered, Missing Items, or Product Quality Issue).
  • 4 Mandatory Order Photos (Min 4 & Max 4 Images): Clear, well-lit photographs in JPG, JPEG, PNG or WEBP format showing the outer packaging, invoice/order label, affected product and visible damage or discrepancy.
  • 1 Mandatory Order Condition Video (Min 1 & Max 1 Video): A short video (up to 1 minute) in MP4, MOV or WEBM format clearly showing the full order condition and issue.

Important Verification Rule: Our Return Order system validates that all 4 images and 1 video are attached before submission so our quality team can verify the full order condition without back-and-forth delays. Never upload or share banking PINs, passwords or OTP codes.

05

How to Submit a Return Order or Complaint (3-Step Process)

You can report an order issue directly from your FreshMart account using our 3-step Return Order workflow:

  1. Open Return Order from Settings: Go to Account & Settings → MY ORDERS & SHOPPING → Return Order (Report issue & return order).
  2. Step 1 — Enter Customer & Order Details: Enter your Customer Name, select or type your Order Number from your order history, enter your 10-digit Mobile Number, Email Address, Order Address and describe the issue in the Complaint Description box, then click Next: Upload Images & Video.
  3. Step 2 — Upload 4 Images & 1 Video: Upload 4 clear photos of the product/packaging and 1 short video showing the order issue. You can preview, remove or replace any file before clicking Next: Review.
  4. Step 3 — Review & Choose Submission Method: Review your complete summary and choose one of two submission options:
    • Option A — Send Complaint (Submit Directly Through FreshMart): Saves your complaint directly in the FreshMart system, generates a unique Complaint ID (e.g. #CMP-XXXXXX), and sets the initial status to Received.
    • Option B — Send via WhatsApp (+91 79883 00872): Opens WhatsApp with your prepared complaint details for manual support verification.
  5. Track Status in My Complaints: Switch to the My Complaints tab inside the Return Order page anytime to track your live complaint status (Received, Processing, Under Review, Approved, Rejected, or Resolved).
06

Admin Review, Verification & Email Notification

Once your complaint is submitted, the FreshMart verification team compares your complaint description, 4 uploaded photos and video against our store packing and delivery records.

  • If Approved (Store or Delivery Fault): If the product was damaged during delivery, wrongly packed or had a verified quality issue from our side, your complaint is marked Approved and an official confirmation email with replacement/refund next steps is sent to your registered Email Address.
  • If Rejected (Post-Delivery / Unsupported Issue): If verification shows the order was delivered accurately in good condition and the issue occurred after delivery, the complaint is marked Rejected and an email explaining the reason is sent to your registered Email Address.
  • Final Resolution: Once the replacement or refund is completed, the complaint status is updated to Resolved in My Complaints and confirmed via email.
07

How Refunds Are Issued

When a refund is approved, the refund method depends on the original payment method, the transaction status and the capabilities of the payment provider.

  • Online prepaid orders: Where supported, refunds are generally initiated through the original payment channel or the applicable payment provider's process.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): If COD is offered, FreshMart may request the minimum information needed to arrange an approved refund through an available method.
  • Partial refunds: If only some items or quantities qualify, the approved refund may be limited to those items and any applicable related amount.
  • Payment not completed: If a payment failed or an order was not successfully charged, a refund may not be necessary. Any pending debit or reversal must be checked against the payment provider's records.
  • Duplicate payment: If you believe you were charged twice for one order, contact support with the order reference and relevant transaction details.

FreshMart will not ask for your UPI PIN, card PIN, banking password or OTP to process a legitimate refund.

Never approve a collect request or enter your payment PIN merely because someone claims it is required to receive a refund.

08

Refund Processing Time

Refund processing has two stages: the time FreshMart takes to review and approve a request, and the time taken by the payment provider or bank to credit the amount.

Stage What it means
Complaint review Depends on the type of complaint, the available information and any verification needed.
Refund approval FreshMart communicates the outcome and initiates an eligible refund through the applicable process.
Bank or payment provider The time taken to display the credit depends on the payment method and provider.

Proposed service target: If FreshMart adopts a target of initiating approved refunds within 5–10 business days, it must be able to meet that target and should publish it only after confirming its actual process. The bank or payment provider may take additional time to display the credit.

These are not guaranteed universal processing times. If a refund appears delayed, contact support with your order number and, if available, the refund reference.

Where a shorter timeline or different remedy is required by applicable law, the legal requirement will take precedence.

09

Partial Refunds, Discounts and Delivery Charges

If a complaint affects only part of an order, FreshMart may assess a partial refund or another appropriate remedy for the affected item or quantity.

The amount will depend on the order details, the nature of the problem, any applicable discount and the remedy required under applicable law.

  • Missing or incorrect items may be assessed separately from items correctly delivered.
  • A discount or offer does not remove applicable rights regarding defective, incorrect or misrepresented products.
  • Delivery charges will be assessed according to the reason for the complaint, the order terms and applicable law.
  • A refund for an entire order will not automatically be assumed when only one item is affected.

The final amount and its calculation should be communicated clearly to the customer.

10

Items That May Not Be Eligible for Change-of-Mind Returns

Some products may not be suitable for ordinary change-of-mind returns because of hygiene, safety, perishability or the nature of the product. Examples may include:

  • Fresh or perishable food products.
  • Opened food or beverage packaging.
  • Personal-care or hygiene products once opened or used.
  • Products damaged after delivery through customer misuse.
  • Items specifically marked as non-returnable before purchase, where lawful.

Important: A non-returnable label does not automatically exclude complaints about an expired, defective, unsafe, damaged, incorrect or misrepresented item. Such cases will be considered according to the facts and applicable law.

Please check the product listing before placing an order. If the listing includes specific return conditions, those conditions should be read together with this policy and applicable legal rights.

11

Order Cancellation and Refunds

If you want to cancel an order, contact FreshMart as soon as possible. Whether cancellation is possible may depend on the order's processing, packing and dispatch status.

  • Before processing: Cancellation may be possible if the order has not progressed too far in fulfilment.
  • After dispatch: Cancellation may be more difficult and may require a delivery or return process.
  • Cancelled prepaid order: If a payment was collected and a refund is due, it will be processed according to the applicable refund process.
  • Failed or unpaid order: If no payment was collected, a refund may not be required, although a pending debit or reversal should be checked.

FreshMart may cancel an order where fulfilment is not possible, such as when an item is unavailable or a material listing error is identified. Any payment collected for an order that is cancelled must be handled according to applicable law and the relevant payment process.

Read our Cancellation Policy for further details.

12

Delivery Problems, Refused Orders and Missing Deliveries

If your order is marked delivered but you have not received it, first check the delivery location and any delivery communication available to you. Contact FreshMart promptly so the delivery record can be reviewed.

If the package is visibly damaged or leaking, take reasonable precautions and avoid consuming or using a product that may be unsafe. Contact support with the available details.

If a delivery attempt fails because the address is incorrect, the recipient is unavailable or access is not possible, the next steps will depend on the order terms and the circumstances.

A refused delivery does not automatically qualify for a full refund in every situation. Any deduction, redelivery charge or refund decision must be consistent with the terms disclosed before purchase and applicable law.

See our Delivery Policy for delivery conditions and service coverage.

13

Exceptions, Fair Use and Consumer Rights

FreshMart may investigate repeated, inconsistent or potentially fraudulent refund requests to protect customers and the store. Decisions should be based on relevant facts, available evidence and the applicable rules.

Examples that may require additional review include repeated reports of the same issue, conflicting order details or evidence that does not correspond to the affected order.

A customer should not be denied a valid complaint solely because other complaints were previously submitted. Each case should be considered fairly.

Nothing in this policy is intended to waive, restrict or replace mandatory rights and remedies available under applicable consumer-protection law.

Where a term in this policy conflicts with a mandatory legal requirement, the applicable legal requirement will prevail.

14

Complaints, Escalation and Disputes

If you disagree with a return or refund decision, contact FreshMart support and provide your order number, the issue details and any additional information you want considered.

We should review the information again where appropriate, explain the decision and tell you about any available next steps.

Please keep relevant order confirmations, payment records and support communications until the issue is resolved.

If a complaint remains unresolved, you may use the consumer grievance or dispute-resolution mechanisms available under applicable law.

Our Terms & Conditions and Legal Notice provide additional information about the website's terms.
