1. Overview

FreshMart aims to handle order-related concerns fairly and responsibly. If a product is damaged, defective, incorrect or missing, customers can contact support to request a review. Available resolutions depend on the circumstances, the order details, the applicable policy and applicable law.

2. Issues You Can Report

You can contact FreshMart about issues such as:

  • A product different from the one ordered.
  • A damaged or defective item.
  • An item missing from the delivered order.
  • A quantity that does not match the order.
  • A payment or order-related issue that needs investigation.

Reporting an issue does not automatically mean a refund or replacement has been approved. Each request will be reviewed on its own facts and under applicable requirements.

3. How to Request a Return or Refund

  1. Keep your order details or order number ready, if available.
  2. Contact FreshMart through phone, email or WhatsApp support.
  3. Explain the issue and identify the affected product.
  4. Provide relevant photos or other information if reasonably requested and available.
  5. Wait for the support team to review your request and communicate the next steps.

Contact details are provided at the end of this page. This page explains the process; it does not itself submit or approve a refund.

4. Reporting Time and Supporting Information

Please report a delivery problem as soon as reasonably possible after discovering it. Prompt reporting can help FreshMart investigate the issue.

Keep the order details and relevant packaging where practical. A photograph may help explain visible damage or an incorrect product, but evidence requirements depend on the case and applicable law. No fixed reporting deadline is established by this page.

5. Fresh and Perishable Grocery Items

Fresh fruits, vegetables, dairy and other perishable products may require special handling because quality can change quickly. Customers should report quality, damage or incorrect-item concerns promptly.

Return arrangements may differ for perishable, opened or hygiene-sensitive products. Any restrictions must be consistent with the actual product policy and applicable consumer rights. A blanket refusal of every return or refund is not established by this page.

6. Review and Available Resolution

After reviewing a request, FreshMart may communicate an appropriate available resolution, which could include a refund, replacement, correction of an order issue or another suitable remedy where applicable.

The available outcome depends on the nature of the issue, product availability, payment status, the actual service process and applicable law. A replacement is not guaranteed when the product is unavailable.

7. Refund Approval and Processing

A refund can be processed only through the actual payment and order-handling process available to FreshMart. If a refund is approved, support should explain the applicable process and provide status information where available.

Processing time can depend on the payment provider and payment method. This policy does not promise a fixed number of days or guarantee a refund before the request has been reviewed.

8. Prepaid and Cash on Delivery Orders

For prepaid orders, any approved refund depends on the payment method and the refund facilities supported by the actual system. The payment provider may require additional processing time.

For Cash on Delivery orders, the available refund method must be confirmed with support. Do not share your card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN or account password to request a refund.

9. Delivery Charges

Whether a delivery charge is refundable depends on the facts of the case, the applicable order terms and applicable law. The final outcome should be communicated when the request is reviewed.

10. Items That May Have Return Restrictions

Certain perishable, opened or hygiene-sensitive items may have restrictions where those restrictions are lawful and disclosed. These restrictions do not remove rights or remedies that apply under mandatory law, including in relevant cases involving damaged, defective or incorrectly supplied products.

11. If Your Request Is Not Approved

If a request cannot be approved, you may contact FreshMart support for clarification or additional review. Please provide the relevant order details and any information that may help explain the issue.

Nothing in this policy prevents you from exercising rights or remedies available under applicable law.

12. Cancellation-Related Refunds

If an order is cancelled, any eligible refund will be handled according to the circumstances, the payment process and the applicable policy. See our Cancellation Policy for additional information.

13. Policy Updates

FreshMart may update this policy when its services, operational processes or legal requirements change. The current version should be published on this page.
