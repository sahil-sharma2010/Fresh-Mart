Refund Policy

We want you to have a reliable shopping experience. This policy explains how to report an order issue, request a refund and understand the next steps. Eligibility depends on the circumstances of your order and applicable consumer rights.

Clear Process Order Issue Support Customer Assistance

Refund policy at a glance

The main points to understand before requesting a refund.

Order Details

Keep your order number and registered contact details ready so support can identify the purchase you are asking about.

Missing or Damaged Items

Report missing, damaged or incorrect products promptly after delivery and include clear details about the issue.

Review and Resolution

Each request may be reviewed using order information and relevant evidence before a suitable resolution is confirmed.

Refund Method

Where a refund is approved, the method and processing time depend on the original payment method and applicable payment provider.

Perishable Products

For groceries and other perishable items, report quality concerns promptly. The available resolution depends on the issue and applicable law.

Customer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove any consumer right or legal protection that applies to your purchase.

How to request a refund

Follow these steps to help us understand and review your request.

01

Contact FreshMart Support

Use the available support channel and provide your order number, the affected product and a short description of the issue.

02

Share Relevant Details

Where helpful, provide clear photographs of damaged or incorrect items and the packaging. Do not share passwords, OTPs or payment PINs.

03

Request Review

Our support process can review the order details and explain the available resolution based on the circumstances.

04

Review the Resolution

If a refund or another resolution is approved, follow the instructions provided by support. Payment providers may need additional time to process a refund.

Important information

FreshMart should publish specific reporting deadlines, eligibility conditions and processing estimates only when they match its actual operating policy. Contact support for guidance on a particular order.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common refund questions.

How can I request a refund?

Contact FreshMart Support with your order number, the product details and a description of the issue. The support team can explain the next steps.

What if an item arrives damaged or is missing?

Report the issue promptly and include the affected item details. Clear photographs may help support review the request.

How long does a refund take?

Processing time depends on the review, payment method and payment provider. Support should communicate the applicable estimate once the refund status is confirmed.

Can grocery or perishable items be refunded?

Quality issues should be reported promptly. The available resolution depends on the circumstances, the applicable policy and your consumer rights.

What if my refund has not arrived?

Contact support with your order details and any refund confirmation you have received. The payment provider may also need time to complete the transaction.

Can I cancel an order instead?

Cancellation depends on the order status and the applicable cancellation rules. Visit the Cancellation Policy or contact support. Read Cancellation Policy →
