Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about FreshMart.

What is FreshMart?

FreshMart is an online grocery shopping website designed to help customers explore products and shop for everyday essentials.

How can I browse products?

Visit the Home page and use the available product categories or search features, if provided, to explore the catalogue.

Does FreshMart deliver to every location?

Delivery availability depends on the service areas supported. Check your address and delivery information on the website before placing an order.

Visit the Delivery Information and Refund Policy pages using the related links below to review the applicable details.
