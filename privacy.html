Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you browse FreshMart, contact us or use shopping features available on our website.

Your information deserves care

FreshMart aims to handle personal information responsibly and use it only for appropriate purposes connected with the website and its services. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes the approach FreshMart takes towards personal information in connection with its website. The exact information processed depends on the features you use, the information you provide and the technical services actually enabled on the website.

This policy should be read with our Terms & Conditions. Applicable privacy and consumer-protection laws continue to apply.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features available and your interactions with FreshMart, relevant information may include:

  • Contact details: Name, phone number and email address that you provide.
  • Delivery information: Address and delivery instructions supplied for an order.
  • Order information: Products, quantities, order status and related support requests.
  • Account information: Details associated with an account, if registration is available.
  • Messages: Information you voluntarily include when contacting customer support.
  • Technical information: Basic browser or device information, logs, cookies or analytics data only where the relevant systems collect them.

FreshMart should collect only information that is reasonably needed for a disclosed purpose. This list does not mean that every type of information is currently collected by the website.

3. How We May Use Information

Where applicable, information may be used to:

  • Process and manage orders.
  • Coordinate delivery and communicate order-related updates.
  • Respond to questions, complaints and support requests.
  • Maintain customer accounts where account features are enabled.
  • Help identify technical errors, misuse or fraudulent activity.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.
  • Improve website usability using technical information where such processing is enabled and appropriate.

Personal information should not be used for an unrelated purpose without an appropriate legal basis or required notice or consent.

4. Orders and Customer Accounts

If you place an order, the details you submit may be needed to process the purchase, coordinate delivery and address issues. If FreshMart offers customer accounts, account information may be used to provide the account features you request.

Please provide accurate information and avoid sharing passwords, verification codes or confidential payment credentials with anyone claiming to be customer support.

5. Payments and Payment Providers

If online payment functionality is enabled, payments may be handled by the payment service provider selected for checkout. The provider may process information according to its own applicable terms and privacy policy.

The exact payment information handled or retained by FreshMart depends on the integration actually implemented. Do not send your card PIN, CVV, UPI PIN or account password through support messages.

6. Cookies and Analytics

The website may use essential browser storage, cookies or analytics technologies if those features are implemented. Their purpose and behavior should be disclosed where required.

This policy does not claim that tracking, advertising analytics or non-essential cookies are active unless they have actually been configured. You can review your browser settings to manage cookies, although disabling some technologies may affect website functionality.

7. How Information May Be Shared

Personal information may be shared where necessary and appropriate with service providers involved in operating requested services, such as:

  • Delivery providers, where delivery is outsourced.
  • Payment providers, where online payment processing is enabled.
  • Hosting or technical service providers that support the website.
  • Competent authorities where disclosure is required by law.

Information should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. FreshMart should not sell personal information or disclose it for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis and required transparency.

8. How We Protect Information

FreshMart aims to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. Access to personal information should be restricted to authorized purposes, and technical and organizational protections should be reviewed as the website develops.

No website, database or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Customers should use strong passwords where accounts are supported and contact FreshMart if they suspect an issue involving their information.

9. How Long Information Is Kept

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its stated purpose, applicable legal obligations, legitimate recordkeeping requirements or dispute resolution. The exact retention period depends on the type of information and the services used.

FreshMart should establish appropriate retention and deletion practices for its actual database, logs, backups and service providers. This policy does not promise automatic deletion unless such a process has been implemented.

10. Your Privacy Choices and Requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may be able to request access to, correction of or deletion of certain personal information, or exercise other applicable privacy rights.

To make a request, contact FreshMart using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

11. Marketing Communications

If FreshMart introduces promotional messages, it should provide appropriate notice and any consent or opt-out mechanism required by applicable law. You may contact support to ask about marketing preferences. Do not assume that promotional messaging is active merely because this policy describes it.

12. Children's Privacy

FreshMart is intended for ordinary grocery shopping by people who are legally able to use the relevant service. Where information relating to a child is involved, applicable requirements for children's data and any required parental consent must be followed.

13. Third-Party Websites

FreshMart may link to external websites or services. Those services may have separate privacy policies and practices. Review their information before submitting personal details to a third-party service.

14. Updates to This Policy

This policy may be revised when FreshMart's features, information practices or legal obligations change. The updated version should be published on this page, with an accurate revision date and additional notice where required by law.
