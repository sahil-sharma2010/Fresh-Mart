Your privacy matters to FreshMart. This policy explains the types of personal information that may be used when you browse our website, create an account, place an order or contact support. We aim to handle information responsibly and transparently.

Personal Information Transparency Privacy Awareness

Privacy at a glance

Important information about privacy when using FreshMart.

Account Information

When you register or use an account, information such as your name, email address and phone number may be used to provide account and shopping services.

Order Information

Details such as ordered products, delivery information and order history may be processed to manage purchases and customer support.

Customer Support

Information you provide when contacting support may be used to understand your question and help resolve the issue.

Website Usage

Basic technical or usage information may be processed where needed to operate, maintain and improve the website.

Service Providers

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to support functions such as delivery, payment processing or website operations.

Your Privacy Choices

You may contact FreshMart to ask questions about your personal information or request assistance with your account, subject to applicable requirements.

How information may be used

Information should be used only for appropriate purposes connected to the service.

Providing shopping services

To create and manage accounts, process orders, coordinate delivery, respond to questions and communicate important updates about purchases.

Maintaining website operations

To troubleshoot issues, protect the website, prevent misuse and maintain a reliable shopping experience.

Improving customer experience

Where applicable, information may help understand service issues and improve website usability. Any optional analytics or marketing practices should be disclosed accurately.

Meeting legal obligations

Information may be retained or disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

Your privacy and account choices

A few important points for managing your information responsibly.

Keep your details accurate

Provide correct account and delivery information and update it when needed.

Protect your account

Keep passwords and verification codes private and sign out of shared devices.

Ask about your information

Contact support with privacy questions or requests relating to your account.

Review policy updates

Check this page occasionally for updates to the published privacy practices.

About privacy requests

Requests to access, correct or delete personal information may be subject to identity verification, record-retention requirements and applicable law. Contact FreshMart Support for guidance.

Sharing, storage and retention

Information sharing

Personal information may be shared with relevant service providers where necessary to fulfil orders, support payments, operate the website or provide customer support. Information should not be shared beyond legitimate purposes without an appropriate basis.

Data protection

FreshMart should use reasonable measures appropriate to its actual systems and operations to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention

Personal information should be kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the relevant purpose, applicable legal obligations, dispute handling and legitimate business records.

Third-party services

External payment, delivery or other linked services may have their own privacy practices. Review their applicable notices when using those services.

Children's privacy

FreshMart is intended for people who can lawfully use the service. If you believe a child's personal information has been provided improperly, contact support for assistance.

Policy changes

This page may be updated when the website's practices or applicable requirements change. The published page should accurately reflect the practices in effect.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common privacy questions.

What personal information may FreshMart use?

Depending on the features you use, this may include account details, order and delivery information, and details you provide when contacting support. The final policy should match the information the website actually collects.

Why is my information needed?

Relevant information may be needed to manage accounts, process orders, coordinate delivery, provide support and maintain the service.

How can I ask about my personal information?

Contact FreshMart Support with your request. You may be asked to verify your identity before account-related information is discussed.

Does this policy cover third-party services?

External services may have their own privacy notices. This policy should explain the third-party services that FreshMart actually uses and how they relate to the shopping experience.

How will I know if the policy changes?

The latest version should be published on this page. Check it periodically for updates relevant to your use of FreshMart.
