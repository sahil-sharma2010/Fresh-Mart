Privacy Policy

At FreshMart, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you browse our store, place orders, manage your cart or contact us, and how that information may be used, stored and protected.

01

Introduction

FreshMart is an online store for groceries, beverages, snacks, household essentials and other daily-use products. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you visit or use our website.

This policy applies to information processed through FreshMart's website and its enabled shopping features. The exact information processed depends on which features you use and which services are active.

By using the website, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this policy. Where consent is legally required for a particular activity, we will seek that consent as applicable.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, FreshMart may process the following categories of information:

  • Contact information: name, mobile number, email address and delivery address when you provide them.
  • Account information: account identifiers and profile details needed to provide account features.
  • Order information: products ordered, quantities, order totals, order history, delivery details and order status.
  • Shopping preferences: cart items, wishlist entries and other shopping selections, if these features are enabled.
  • Support communications: information you include in messages or requests sent to us.
  • Technical information: information such as browser type, device information, timestamps and basic diagnostic data where collected by the website or its service providers.
  • Payment status: transaction references, payment method information or payment status where required to process or reconcile an order.

We do not need your account password, payment PIN or one-time verification code through ordinary customer-support messages. Never send these credentials to anyone claiming to represent FreshMart.

Please provide only information needed for the service you are using.

03

How Information Is Collected

Information may be obtained in the following ways:

  • When you enter information into forms or account fields.
  • When you add products to a cart or wishlist.
  • When you submit an order or provide delivery information.
  • When you contact us for assistance.
  • Through essential website technologies and service providers, where applicable.

Some information is necessary to complete an order or provide a requested feature. If you do not provide required information, we may be unable to provide that service.

04

How We Use Information

FreshMart may use relevant information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts, where available.
  • Processing orders and coordinating delivery.
  • Maintaining cart, wishlist and shopping features.
  • Communicating order confirmations and important updates.
  • Responding to customer questions and complaints.
  • Maintaining website functionality and troubleshooting errors.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, unauthorized access and misuse.
  • Meeting applicable legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • Improving our services using appropriate information and safeguards.

Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using personal information for a purpose that requires consent. Optional marketing communications should be distinguishable from essential order-related messages.

05

Orders, Accounts & Shopping Data

When you use shopping features, relevant information may be processed to maintain your cart, wishlist, profile and orders. If these features are connected to an account, the information may be associated with that account.

Order details may be used to fulfil purchases, confirm payment status, coordinate delivery, handle cancellations and manage eligible refunds or customer-support requests.

Please check your delivery address and contact information before submitting an order. Contact us promptly if you notice an error.

Our Terms & Conditions, Delivery Policy, Return & Refund Policy and Cancellation Policy explain the relevant shopping rules.

06

Payments & Transaction Security

Payment processing depends on the payment options enabled on the website. If a third-party payment provider is used, that provider may process payment information under its own terms and privacy policy.

FreshMart should collect or retain only the payment information necessary for its actual payment workflow. Do not store full card details, card security codes, UPI PINs or payment passwords in ordinary website tables, order notes or customer profiles.

A payment provider may return a transaction identifier or payment status so an order can be reconciled. Such information should be handled with appropriate access controls.

The exact payment data handled by FreshMart depends on the payment integration in use. This policy does not imply that a particular payment provider or payment method is currently enabled.

07

When We Share Information

We may disclose relevant information when reasonably necessary for the following purposes:

  • Service providers: hosting, database, website operations, communications, delivery or payment services used to provide the requested service.
  • Legal requirements: responding to valid legal obligations or lawful requests by competent authorities.
  • Safety and security: investigating suspected fraud, misuse, security incidents or threats to users.
  • Business changes: handling a potential business transfer or restructuring, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Information should be shared only to the extent reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and subject to applicable safeguards.

FreshMart should not sell personal information as a business practice. If the website uses advertising, analytics or other tracking services, their operation and any required consent must be disclosed accurately.

08

Cookies, Local Storage & Preferences

The website may use cookies, browser local storage or similar technologies to support features such as shopping carts, saved preferences, session management and security.

Which technologies are used depends on the features implemented on the website. Browser storage can remain on your device until it expires or you clear it, depending on the technology.

Clearing browser storage may remove saved cart items or preferences that have not been stored in your account or database.

Where non-essential cookies or tracking technologies require consent, they should not be activated before the required consent is obtained.

09

Data Security & Database Protection

We should use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

Appropriate measures may include:

  • Using HTTPS for website connections.
  • Restricting access to customer and order information.
  • Using database permissions and access policies.
  • Keeping passwords and secret keys out of public website code.
  • Reviewing access permissions and security settings.
  • Keeping necessary software and services updated.

If FreshMart uses Supabase or another database service, database access should be restricted according to the user's permissions. Public website code must not contain service-role keys, private credentials or other secrets that grant unrestricted access.

Security measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal-data incident occurs, applicable notification and response obligations will be followed.

Read our Security Policy for additional information.

10

Data Retention & Deletion

We should retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including order fulfilment, customer support, security and applicable legal or accounting requirements.

Retention periods may differ for account records, order history, transaction records, support communications and technical logs. The period depends on the purpose, applicable requirements and the services being used.

When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate, subject to lawful retention obligations and technical limitations.

Deleting an account or removing a record from the visible website does not necessarily remove every backup, transaction record or copy held by a service provider immediately.

11

Your Privacy Rights & Choices

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may be able to request:

  • Information about personal data processed about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Help with applicable complaints or grievance procedures.

These rights may be subject to identity verification, legal requirements, exceptions and legitimate retention needs.

To make a privacy request, use the support or contact channel provided on the FreshMart website. Include enough information for us to understand and verify your request, but do not send passwords, payment PINs or verification codes.

If you are in India, applicable data-protection rights and obligations depend on the laws and provisions in force and applicable to the circumstances.

12

Children's Privacy

FreshMart is a general shopping website and is not intended to encourage children to provide personal information independently where parental involvement or consent is required.

Where applicable law requires parental consent or additional safeguards for a child's personal information, those requirements must be followed.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

13

Third-Party Websites & Services

FreshMart may link to external websites or use third-party services for hosting, databases, payments, communications or other functions.

External websites and service providers may have their own privacy policies, terms and security practices. We encourage you to review those policies when using their services.

A link to an external service does not, by itself, mean that FreshMart operates or controls that service.

14

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when website features, data practices, service providers or applicable legal requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date where appropriate.

We encourage you to review this page periodically so you can understand how information is handled.

15

Contact & Privacy Requests

For questions about this Privacy Policy, requests relating to your personal information, or privacy concerns, please use the contact details or support options published on the FreshMart website.
