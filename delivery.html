1. Introduction

This Delivery Policy explains the general conditions that may apply when ordering products through FreshMart. Actual delivery availability, charges and options depend on the services currently offered and the information shown during checkout.

2. Delivery Coverage

FreshMart can deliver only to locations covered by its available delivery service. A delivery request may depend on the address, service availability, product stock and operational conditions.

Please enter your correct delivery address and check the availability information shown on the website before placing an order. Do not assume that every city or pin code is serviceable.

3. Order Processing

After an order is submitted, its details may need to be checked before the order is prepared. Processing depends on the order information, product availability, payment status where relevant, and operational conditions.

An order submission message does not by itself guarantee that every item is available or that delivery has been completed. Use the actual order status provided by FreshMart, if available.

4. Estimated Delivery Time

Any delivery estimate displayed at checkout or communicated by FreshMart should be treated as an estimate unless expressly confirmed otherwise. Timing may vary based on location, stock, order volume, traffic, weather and other circumstances.

FreshMart does not promise a fixed delivery time through this policy unless a specific commitment is explicitly displayed for the relevant order.

5. Delivery Charges and Minimum Order

Any applicable delivery charge, minimum order requirement or additional fee should be disclosed before the customer confirms the order, where applicable. Please review the final checkout summary carefully.

This policy does not set a fixed delivery fee or minimum order amount. Only the amounts actually configured and displayed by FreshMart should apply.

6. Delivery Address and Customer Availability

  • Provide a complete and accurate delivery address.
  • Keep the contact details provided with the order up to date.
  • Where possible, ensure that you or an authorized receiver is available at the delivery location.
  • Inform support promptly if you notice an address error.

Incorrect details or an unavailable receiver may affect delivery. Any further delivery arrangements will depend on the options FreshMart actually supports.

7. Order Status and Tracking

If FreshMart provides a working order-status or tracking feature, use that feature to review the latest available information. Status updates may not always appear instantly.

If tracking is not available for your order, contact Help & Support with your order details. This page does not create a tracking service or guarantee live location updates.

8. Delivery Delays

Delays can occur because of weather, traffic, high order volume, product availability, address problems or other operational circumstances. If a delay affects your order, contact FreshMart for available information and assistance.

Nothing in this section removes any rights or remedies available to customers under applicable law.

9. Unavailable or Out-of-Stock Products

Product availability can change between browsing and order processing. If an item cannot be supplied, FreshMart should communicate the available options where reasonably possible.

Depending on the actual ordering system and circumstances, an affected item or order may require an update, cancellation or eligible refund. A substitute should not be assumed unless accepted by the customer where required.

10. Missing, Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives with a missing, damaged, defective or incorrect item, contact FreshMart and provide the relevant order details and a clear description of the issue.

Keep any relevant packaging or supporting information where reasonably possible. Requests will be reviewed under the applicable policy and law.

See our Return & Refund Policy for further guidance.

11. Failed or Unsuccessful Delivery

Delivery may be unsuccessful if the address is incorrect, no authorized receiver is available, access is restricted or another delivery issue occurs. Contact FreshMart to understand the available next steps for your order.

Re-delivery charges, automatic cancellation or other consequences should not be assumed unless they are part of the actual disclosed policy applicable to the order.

12. Cancellation and Refunds

Order cancellation depends on the order status, available functionality, applicable policy and law. Eligible refunds depend on the circumstances and the actual payment process.

Read our Cancellation Policy and Return & Refund Policy for additional information.

13. Changes to This Policy

FreshMart may update this policy when delivery services, operational processes or applicable requirements change. The latest version should be published on this page.
