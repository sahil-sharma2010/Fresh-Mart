Delivery Policy

FreshMart aims to deliver your everyday essentials through a clear and convenient ordering process. This policy explains delivery availability, order processing, estimated timelines, delivery charges, address requirements and how we handle delivery problems.

01

Policy Overview

This Delivery Policy explains how orders placed through FreshMart may be processed and delivered. It applies to orders accepted through our website, subject to the details displayed at checkout.

Delivery availability, delivery charges, estimated arrival times and fulfilment options may depend on your location, product availability, order size and the delivery service available for your order.

Before placing an order: Check your delivery address, contact details, product quantities, final order total, delivery charges and any delivery estimate displayed at checkout.

The information shown for your particular order should be read together with this policy, our Terms & Conditions and our Return & Refund Policy.

02

Delivery Areas and Availability

FreshMart delivers only to locations covered by the delivery service currently available for the store. Availability may differ by area, postal code, distance, stock and operational capacity.

A product being visible on the website does not necessarily mean it can be delivered to every address.

  • Delivery eligibility should be checked using the location or address options provided by the website, where available.
  • Some products may have additional delivery restrictions because of their size, handling needs or availability.
  • Service coverage may change because of operational conditions.
  • An order may require review if the entered address is outside the supported delivery area.

If delivery to your address is unavailable, we will not promise a delivery time that the service cannot reasonably support. If a paid order cannot be fulfilled, the available cancellation or refund remedy will be handled according to applicable law and the order circumstances.

03

Placing and Confirming Orders

To place an order, select the products you want, confirm their quantities and provide the information requested during checkout.

Before submitting an order, review the product names, variants, quantities, prices, applicable charges and final payable amount.

An order request may be subject to stock availability, payment status, address verification and confirmation through the website's ordering process.

A checkout submission or payment initiation does not necessarily mean the order has been accepted or dispatched. Check the order status or confirmation message provided by FreshMart.

If an order cannot be fulfilled because an item is unavailable or an important listing error is identified, FreshMart may contact you regarding an appropriate resolution.

04

Order Processing and Dispatch

After an order is submitted, processing may include checking the order details, confirming stock, preparing products, packing the order and arranging delivery.

Processing time is not always the same as delivery time. An order may require additional time if:

  • A product is temporarily unavailable.
  • Order details or the delivery address need clarification.
  • Payment confirmation is pending, where applicable.
  • The order requires special handling or additional preparation.
  • Unexpected operational conditions affect fulfilment.

We aim to process accepted orders within the time reasonably supported by the store's operating arrangements. A specific processing commitment applies only where it has been clearly communicated for the order.

05

Estimated Delivery Time

Delivery estimates may depend on your location, order processing, product availability, delivery capacity, traffic, weather and other operational factors.

If an estimated delivery window is displayed at checkout or in an order confirmation, use that estimate for the relevant order. Unless expressly stated otherwise, an estimate is not a guarantee of arrival at an exact time.

Important: FreshMart should publish a fixed delivery promise, such as same-day or next-day delivery, only when the store actually supports it for the selected address and order.

If a delivery estimate changes materially, FreshMart should communicate an update through an available contact or order-status channel where reasonably possible.

If the delay makes the order unsuitable for your needs, contact support to discuss the available options. Any cancellation or refund will be assessed under the relevant policy and applicable law.

06

Delivery Charges, Minimum Orders and Offers

Delivery charges, if applicable, should be displayed during checkout before you submit the order.

The total payable amount may include product prices, applicable taxes, delivery charges and other charges that are lawfully applicable and clearly disclosed before the order is placed.

  • Delivery fee: The charge, if any, shown for your order.
  • Minimum order: A minimum purchase amount may apply only if the requirement is displayed before checkout.
  • Free delivery: An offer may be subject to disclosed eligibility conditions, such as order value, location or promotion dates.
  • Discounts: Promotional offers are subject to their displayed terms and validity.

If a fee or offer is displayed incorrectly, the order should be reviewed fairly and handled according to the information presented at checkout, applicable law and the circumstances.

FreshMart should not add an undisclosed charge after an order is submitted without an appropriate lawful basis and customer authorization where required.

07

Delivery Address and Contact Details

Customers are responsible for entering accurate delivery information, including the recipient's name, address, postal code, mobile number and useful delivery instructions where requested.

Incorrect or incomplete details may prevent a delivery from being completed on time.

  • Review your address before placing the order.
  • Include apartment, floor, house number or landmark where useful.
  • Keep the provided contact number reachable around delivery time.
  • Contact support promptly if you notice an address error after placing an order.

Address changes may not be possible once an order has been packed or dispatched. A change may require additional verification or a new delivery arrangement.

FreshMart will handle address and contact information according to its Privacy Policy.

08

Order Status and Tracking

Where order-status features are available, customers may be able to view updates such as order placed, processing, packed, dispatched, out for delivery, delivered or cancelled.

The exact statuses depend on the functionality implemented on the FreshMart website.

A status update may not always be instantaneous. Technical issues or communication delays can cause a status to appear later than the actual event.

If an order status appears incorrect, or an order is marked delivered but has not arrived, contact FreshMart support with your order number so the details can be checked.

FreshMart will not claim that live GPS tracking or automated delivery notifications are available unless those features are actually implemented.

09

Delivery Attempts and Receiving an Order

Please ensure that someone authorized to receive the order is available at the delivery address during the communicated delivery window, where possible.

The delivery person may need to confirm the recipient or delivery details before handing over the package.

If the recipient is unavailable, the address is inaccessible or the delivery cannot be completed, FreshMart may contact the customer to discuss a reasonable next step.

Depending on the service available, the next step may involve another delivery attempt, an agreed alternative arrangement or cancellation where appropriate.

Any additional delivery fee, cancellation deduction or other charge must be disclosed and applied consistently with the order terms and applicable law.

10

Delivery Delays and Unexpected Disruptions

Delivery may be delayed by circumstances such as heavy traffic, severe weather, public restrictions, local disruptions, operational problems, product shortages or service-provider interruptions.

When a delay occurs, FreshMart will aim to provide relevant information through available communication channels where reasonably possible.

If fulfilment becomes impossible, FreshMart may need to cancel the affected order and arrange any applicable refund in accordance with the order circumstances and applicable law.

Nothing in this section removes mandatory consumer rights or excuses obligations that remain applicable under law.

11

Checking Your Order at Delivery

When receiving an order, please check the package and products where reasonably possible.

  • Check whether the delivered items match your order.
  • Check quantities and visible packaging damage.
  • Review expiry or use-by labels where applicable.
  • Keep order confirmation and packaging until you are satisfied.
  • Report incorrect, missing, damaged, expired or unsafe products promptly.

If a product appears contaminated, leaking, unsafe or otherwise unsuitable for use, do not consume or use it. Keep it away from children and follow appropriate safety precautions.

If you discover a problem after the delivery, contact support. A signature or delivery confirmation does not automatically remove rights relating to hidden defects, incorrect products or other issues recognized by applicable law.

For remedies, refer to our Return & Refund Policy.

12

Failed, Missing or Incorrect Deliveries

If an order is marked delivered but you have not received it, contact FreshMart promptly. We may review the order status, delivery details and relevant service records.

If an order is incomplete, report the missing products and provide the order number and item details.

If the wrong order or wrong products are delivered, keep the relevant information available and contact support for guidance.

Depending on the investigation and applicable rules, the resolution may include completing the delivery, replacing an eligible item, refunding an eligible amount or another appropriate remedy.

We may ask reasonable questions to investigate a report, but each case should be assessed fairly based on its circumstances.

13

Delivery Cancellation and Refunds

If you wish to cancel an order, contact FreshMart as soon as possible. Cancellation may depend on whether the order has been accepted, processed, packed or dispatched.

If FreshMart cannot deliver an accepted, paid order, the available resolution will depend on the reason, order status and applicable legal requirements.

Where a refund is due, its processing depends on the original payment method and the relevant payment provider. The time for the amount to appear in your account may vary.

A failed payment, pending bank debit or duplicate transaction may require verification before the appropriate correction or reversal can be confirmed.

Read our Cancellation Policy and Return & Refund Policy for more details.

14

Customer Responsibilities and Delivery Safety

Customers should use accurate order information and cooperate reasonably with delivery arrangements.

  • Provide a complete and correct delivery address.
  • Keep the delivery contact number reachable where possible.
  • Follow any reasonable instructions required for safe delivery.
  • Do not ask a delivery person to perform unsafe or unlawful activities.
  • Report suspicious payment requests or impersonation attempts through the store's support channel.

FreshMart should handle customer information responsibly and use it only for appropriate purposes connected with the order and applicable legal obligations.

Review our Privacy Policy and Security Policy for further information.

15

Delivery Complaints and Customer Support

If you have a delivery question or complaint, use the contact details or support options published on the FreshMart website.

To help us investigate, include your order number, delivery date, a description of the problem and relevant supporting information where available.

Visit Help & Support

FreshMart aims to review delivery complaints fairly and explain the available next steps. If a complaint remains unresolved, customers may use the grievance or consumer-dispute mechanisms available under applicable law.

FreshMart may update this Delivery Policy when its services, delivery arrangements or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page.

For general website terms, read our Terms & Conditions.
