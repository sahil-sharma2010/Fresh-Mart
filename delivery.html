Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common delivery questions.

How can I check whether FreshMart delivers to my area?

Enter your delivery address during checkout. Availability depends on the areas served by FreshMart. If your address is not accepted, contact support for clarification.

How long will my delivery take?

The estimated delivery time, if provided, should be checked during checkout or in your order updates. Timing can vary depending on location, product availability and operating conditions.

Are delivery charges applicable?

Any applicable delivery charge or additional fee should be displayed in the order summary before you confirm your purchase. Review the final amount at checkout.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Address changes may not be possible after an order has been processed or dispatched. Contact support as soon as possible and provide your order details. Changes are subject to confirmation.

What should I do if my order is delayed?

Check the latest order status first. If the status is unavailable or the order appears delayed, contact FreshMart support with your order number and registered contact details.

What if my order is marked delivered but I have not received it?

Check whether someone at your address has received the order. If you still cannot locate it, contact support promptly with your order number so the issue can be reviewed.

What if an item is missing or damaged?

Contact support and share your order number along with a clear description of the issue. For damaged items, photos may help with the review. Any resolution will follow the applicable policy.

Can I cancel an order that is being delivered?

Cancellation depends on the current order status and the applicable cancellation policy. Please review the cancellation page and contact support as soon as possible.

Need Help With Your Delivery?

Contact FreshMart support and include your order number and a short description of the issue. Do not share passwords or OTPs.
