Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common FreshMart customer questions.

How do I get help with an order?

Contact support with your order number, registered contact details and a short description of the issue. Never share your password or OTP.

How can I report a missing or damaged product?

Contact support promptly after delivery. Mention the affected product and provide clear photos if they help explain the issue. See our Refund Policy for more information.

How do I ask about a refund?

Share your order details with support so the request can be reviewed. Any applicable resolution and processing time depend on the order, payment method and relevant policy.

Can I cancel my order?

Cancellation depends on the order status and applicable conditions. Read the Cancellation Policy or contact support for help with a specific order.

How can I keep my account safe?

Use a strong password, keep verification codes private and avoid suspicious links. Visit the Security Center for more tips.

How can I ask about my personal information?

Contact support with your privacy question. You may need to verify your identity for account-related requests. Read our Privacy Policy.
