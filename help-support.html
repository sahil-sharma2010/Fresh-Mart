01

Support Overview

FreshMart provides this Help & Support page to guide customers who have questions about shopping, orders or using the website.

Use the contact details or support options actually published on the FreshMart website. This page does not create a live chat, telephone hotline or guaranteed response time unless that service is separately provided.

Before contacting support: Keep your order number, a brief description of the issue and any relevant order confirmation available. Never share your password, OTP or banking PIN.

02

Orders and Order Status

If you have a question about an order, first check any order confirmation or status information available on the website.

  • Review the products and quantities in your order.
  • Check the order number and confirmation details.
  • Confirm whether payment was completed, if applicable.
  • Contact support if the order status appears incorrect or the order cannot be located.

Live order tracking and automated status notifications are available only if those features have been implemented.

03

Payment Questions

If a payment is pending, failed or appears duplicated, retain the relevant transaction reference and check your payment provider's status.

Do not repeat a payment without first checking whether the original transaction completed, where that information is available.

  • Keep the order reference and transaction ID.
  • Never send a card PIN, banking password or OTP to support.
  • For an unauthorized transaction, contact your bank or payment provider promptly.

Payment reversals and bank processing times depend on the payment provider and transaction circumstances.

04

Delivery Problems

Contact support if an order is late, marked delivered but not received, incomplete or delivered to the wrong address.

Provide the order reference, delivery date and a clear description of the issue. Photos may help with damaged or incorrect items where relevant.

For delivery terms, read the Delivery Policy. Resolution may depend on the order details and applicable law.

05

Cancellation Requests

If you want to cancel an order, contact FreshMart as soon as possible using the available support channel.

Whether cancellation is possible may depend on whether the order is processing, packed, dispatched or delivered. Wait for confirmation rather than assuming your request was accepted.

Read the Cancellation Policy for more information. This support page does not itself cancel orders automatically.

06

Refund Questions

If you are waiting for a refund, keep your order reference and any cancellation or refund confirmation.

  • Check the original payment method.
  • Review any status provided by the payment provider.
  • Contact support if a confirmed refund appears delayed.

Refund eligibility depends on the order circumstances and applicable policy and law. Read the Return & Refund Policy.

07

Product Issues and Complaints

Contact support if a product is missing, damaged, incorrect, expired or appears unsafe.

Keep the order information and any relevant packaging or photographs where reasonably possible. Do not consume or use a product you believe is unsafe.

FreshMart should assess reports fairly and explain the available next steps according to the circumstances, applicable policy and law.

08

Account and Login Problems

If you cannot sign in, check that you are using the correct registered details and the official FreshMart website.

If the problem continues, use the available account-recovery process or contact support. Account recovery may require reasonable verification.

Never share your password or OTP with support staff or anyone claiming to recover your account on your behalf.

09

Password and Account Recovery

If a Forgot Password option is available, use it to request password-reset instructions. For email-based recovery, check your registered inbox and spam folder.

If you have lost access to your registered email or mobile number, contact support through the published channel and explain the situation.

Password-reset email, OTP verification and registered-contact changes must be supported by the actual account system before they can work.

Read the Security Policy for account-safety guidance.

10

Website and Technical Problems

If a page fails to load or a feature does not work, try refreshing the page and checking your internet connection.

  • Use an up-to-date browser.
  • Check that the website address is correct.
  • Try again later if the service appears temporarily unavailable.
  • Describe the issue to support, including the page and the action that caused it.

Do not share passwords, payment credentials or private account data when reporting technical problems.

11

Submitting a Complaint

Customers may report unresolved issues using the support contact options published by FreshMart.

A useful complaint includes a clear summary of the issue, relevant dates and an order reference where applicable.

FreshMart aims to review complaints fairly and explain the available next steps. The outcome will depend on the facts, applicable policies and legal requirements.

12

Contacting FreshMart Support

Use the contact details, support form or other assistance options actually published on the FreshMart website.

Return to FreshMart Home

No specific phone number, email address or operating hours are stated here because those details should match the contact information genuinely provided by the store.

Never trust an unsolicited message asking for your password, OTP or bank details in exchange for support.

13

Information to Include in a Request

To help support understand the issue, provide only information relevant to the request.

  • Order number, if the issue concerns an order.
  • Date of purchase or delivery, where relevant.
  • A short explanation of the issue and the result you are seeking.
  • Relevant screenshots or product photographs, if useful and safe to share.
  • A contact method through which support can respond.

Never include: passwords, OTPs, full payment-card details, banking PINs or unrelated sensitive information.

14

Response Times and Resolution

Response times may depend on the available support channel, the complexity of the request and the information needed to investigate it.

FreshMart should provide an estimated response time only when it can reasonably support that commitment. This page does not promise a fixed resolution deadline.

If further information is required, support may request relevant details while taking reasonable care with customer information.

15

Customer Rights and Related Policies

Nothing on this page removes customer rights available under applicable law. If an issue remains unresolved, customers may use the consumer grievance or dispute-resolution mechanisms available to them.
