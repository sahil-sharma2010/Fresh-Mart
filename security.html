Your shopping security

Important practices that help protect your FreshMart account and shopping experience.

Account Protection

Use a strong, unique password for your account. Keep your login details private and sign out when using a shared device.

OTP Safety

Never share a verification code with anyone. Only use an OTP when you have requested the related sign-in or account action.

Payment Awareness

Review the final amount before confirming an order. Use trusted payment methods and never disclose your card PIN or banking password.

Personal Information

Provide only the information needed for your account and orders. Review our Privacy Policy to understand how personal information is handled.

Fraud Awareness

Be cautious of unexpected messages asking for money, OTPs, passwords or sensitive information while claiming to represent FreshMart.

Safe Device Usage

Keep your browser and device updated. Avoid saving passwords on shared computers and secure your device when you leave it unattended.

Important security rules

Remember these points whenever you shop online.

Never share confidential verification details

Keep your passwords, OTPs, card PINs and banking credentials private. Do not follow suspicious payment links or requests from unknown senders.

Check before you click

Review links and sender details before opening unexpected messages.

Review your orders

Check product details, delivery address and total amount before checkout.

Protect your password

Do not reuse your FreshMart password on unrelated websites.

Report suspicious activity

Contact support if you notice unexpected account activity or a suspicious order.
